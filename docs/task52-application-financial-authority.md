## Conservative inference accounting

The fictional payload fixes provider/model, system text, canonical Interpretation schema, source, output cap, no reasoning, no tools, no caching and `store=false`. The counter receives complete canonical bytes, checks the protected schema/prompt/model policy and records their digest and byte count. It does not persist source plaintext. This `fake-utf8-complete-v1` count is an intentionally fictional billing unit, **not real tokenization or proof of an actual provider count**. SQL parameter logging and data privacy must be reviewed before real input reaches any hosted counting service.

Protected pricing derives input charge plus the maximum authorized output charge before dispatch, using numeric intermediates and bounded integers. Worker-supplied prices, extra cost categories, unsupported models, altered schemas/prompts and excessive tokens refuse. Every admitted operation remains consumed even if its synthetic price is zero. Real pricing profiles need verified full-input counting, exact model revision, billing units/rounding, output/reasoning/caching/tools and failed-request semantics; unknown bounds must refuse.

Reported usage is an append-only observation, separately labelled from the conservative reservation. A consistent report may calculate a lower reported charge but releases **zero** reserved exposure. Missing, malformed, substituted, excessive or contradictory usage cannot authorize a request retrospectively or refund it. Failure/uncertainty retains the reservation and stops the grant; no automatic resends. Success requires complete durable delivery intent and valid LLM usage. Pending deterministic clarification uses existing source evidence and does not buy a second inference.

## Independent witness and recovery

`grant_witness.sql` runs in a second disposable PostgreSQL database/container with a separate volume and separate reader/service/operator roles. The worker cannot enroll or rotate an epoch; no implicit epoch exists. Operator epoch events are append-only and cannot rotate back to an earlier identity. Rotation invalidates the old configured grant/sessions and quarantines the full at-risk grant; reconciliation records can leave it stopped, never automatically refund or renew it.

The sequence is:

1. Verify current external epoch, source, SQL lease and decrypted Redis evidence.
2. Under the witness-wide lock, compare the complete committed witness-prefix identities with operational facts.
3. Independently price and durably claim the conservative reservation in the witness.
4. Install an issuer-only exact permit; atomically consume/reserve in operational SQL; confirm the mirrored fact externally.
5. Commit operational INTENT. Independently claim and mirror the single-use dispatch and delivery intents before fake I/O.
6. The current call stack alone may execute that fake transport. Record usage/outcome and mirror the terminal outcome into the independent prefix; retain gross charge. Restart cannot reconstruct a permission to resend.

An unfinished cross-database phase fails closed; the two databases are not falsely described as one atomic transaction. Missing or altered financial prefixes, unconfirmed claims and the tested one-component financial-history rollbacks quarantine the full grant, not only the visible restored balance. External serialization complements the existing SQL locks; it does not replace database caps or loosen the250ms operational lock timeout. Availability is deliberately sacrificed when financial safety is uncertain. The local witness lock also serializes fake delivery callbacks, so this prototype demonstrates a conservative concurrency ceiling rather than hosted parallel-dispatch throughput. Witness-first rejection after a durable claim may retain extra conservative exposure and stop the grant; it never grants an automatic financial refund.

### Trust boundary and limitations

Both local databases still share one VM/host and test administrator. Synthetic role separation proves permission enforcement, not independent real human/hosted service isolation. A superuser, compromised privileged verifier/dispatcher/counter, correlated rollback of **both** authority databases, or access to backups/key management outside this model can defeat local isolation. Witness-only rollback that loses an epoch/revocation event without losing any mirrored financial fact can also be undetectable: the independent witness itself is the trusted recovery root here. Hosted controls must prohibit or externally detect that rollback; the tested witness-only financial-prefix rollback is not proof against every witness-administrator restore. A compromised issuer can consume/deny its bounded allowance; financial bounds do not certify model semantics. No tamper-proof hardware, distributed consensus, power-loss durability or region failure claim is made.

Before hosting, independently controlled workload identities, epoch storage/backup policy, authenticated issuer/counter/dispatcher services, durable external delivery evidence, operator reconciliation and tested recovery procedures are mandatory. The independent witness must not be restored together with the operational database without a trusted epoch change and full at-risk quarantine. Local witness readers are explicit injected dependencies, not a hosted enrollment implementation. Full-prefix comparisons and fresh SQL connections add substantial local emulation overhead; these tests do not meet or establish the application’s five-second end-to-end objective. Bounded pooling, prefix-scaling and hosted latency need separate verification. A charged operation with no terminal outcome stays consumed and blocks its root; an explicitly uncertain outcome or restore disagreement quarantines the whole grant. Real provider dispatch remains absent.
